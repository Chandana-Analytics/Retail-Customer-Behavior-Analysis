# Project Overview

- I worked on this project using the Online Retail II dataset to understand customer purchasing behavior. I wanted to explore how often customers place orders, how much they spend, how many products they purchase, and when they last made a purchase.

- I started by exploring the dataset and checking for data quality issues. During profiling, I looked for missing Customer IDs, cancelled invoices, negative quantities, negative prices, and other issues that could affect the analysis. After that, I cleaned the data in Snowflake using SQL and prepared it for further analysis.

- Once the data was cleaned, I analyzed customer purchasing patterns through purchase frequency, customer frequency segmentation, spending, purchase volume, and recency analysis. I also performed RFM segmentation to group customers based on their purchasing behavior and revenue contribution analysis to understand how spending was distributed across customer groups.

- Through this project, I got a better understanding of customer purchasing patterns, customer segments, and spending differences. I also learned how to work with a large retail dataset, handle data quality issues, and use SQL to find meaningful insights.

- The spending figures represent gross customer spending from retained transactions with positive quantities and positive prices. Cancelled invoice rows and negative-quantity return transactions were excluded, although some original purchases associated with cancelled invoices may remain. Therefore, the figures are not net revenue after returns and cancellations.
